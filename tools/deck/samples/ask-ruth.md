# Ask Ruth for a second opinion
Honest design feedback in seconds.

---
## Why teams use it
- Grounded in design principles, not taste
- Works with your Figma links: figma.com/design/file
- Explains the reasons, so you can share them
- Checks contrast, spacing and hierarchy
- Speaks your team's language

## How it works
Upload a screen or paste a Figma link. Ask a question about contrast, spacing or hierarchy. Ruth answers with clear reasons you can share with the team.

The answer names the principle behind it, so the next review goes faster. You can ask a follow-up, compare two versions, or keep a short list of what to fix before the handoff.

Nothing is posted anywhere: the screen stays in your project until you share it yourself.

## Auf Deutsch
Die Barrierefreiheitsanforderungen gelten für jede Benutzeroberflächenkomponente. Ruth prüft Kontraste, Abstände und Hierarchie, z.B. bei Formularen.
